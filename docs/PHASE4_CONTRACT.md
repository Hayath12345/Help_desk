# Phase 4 — Admin panel: build contract (binding)

> **Built 2026-09-30.** Where the implementation refines this contract (`updatedAt` on holidays and business hours,
> the extra guards `escalation_team_archived` and `chain_order`, categories with an archived team left off the form,
> rejected sign-ups that can still be approved, the e2e teardown matching the spec's exact key shape rather than the
> `e2e-` prefix, audit-log times in `business.timezone`), `docs/PHASE4_HANDOFF.md` → "Rules as built" is
> authoritative.

Scope (spec `docs/BUILD_SPEC.md` §14.4 with §5, §10, §11, §12): the admin panel at `/admin` for Super Admins, with a
limited view for Team Leads — the eleven sections of spec §10 plus **Staff** and **General settings** (below), the
audit log, and live config refresh. **Done when:** adding a new category (with its first sub-type) and a custom field
in admin appears on an **already open** New Ticket form in another browser without a reload, and old tickets still
render archived categories, sub-types, custom fields, options, teams and sites (automated E2E + API tests).

Out of scope: reports and the portal help-center page (Phase 5), AWS packaging (Phase 6), the Phase 3 carry-overs
that are not admin work (organisation-level P1 create limit, a sweeper for stuck deliveries, a digest watermark).

Read first: `docs/PHASE3_HANDOFF.md`, `docs/PHASE2_HANDOFF.md`, README, `apps/web/AGENTS.md` (Next.js in this repo is
newer than most training data: read `node_modules/next/dist/docs` before adding routes).

| E4  | **Keys** are generated from the English label (slug, collision suffix, archived rows included), editable while creating, **immutable afterwards**.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| E5  | **Archive, never delete** config (spec §6). Restoring an archived row is the way back (unique keys, holiday dates and escalation steps include archived rows). Guards: a team can't be archived while active categories route to it, it has open tickets, or active escalation rules / on-call reference it; the security category (`tickets.securityCategoryKey`) and its last active sub-type can't be archived; a category is created **with its first sub-type** (a category without an active sub-type never appears on the form, and the list says why).                             |
| E6  | **Old tickets keep rendering archived items**: the ticket detail carries `isActive` for category, sub-type, team and site, and custom-field values with their field label and, for dropdowns, the option label (options are archived per option, never removed while in use). The console panel and the portal header show an "archived" marker; the console shows custom-field values (today nothing does). Tickets on archived teams stay workable (members of an archived team still listed for existing tickets).                                                                      |
| E7  | **Team Lead limited view** (spec §5): sections **Teams & members** and **Macros** only, and only for teams they lead: add/remove **members** (active staff; LEAD role changes are Super Admin only), set the team's **on-call user** (a member with a valid phone), and manage their teams' macros (never global macros, never move a macro to another team or to global). Team email list, Teams webhook, archive — Super Admin only. The API enforces all of it; the web hides what the API would refuse.                                                                                |
| E8  | **Navigation groups** — _Ticket setup_: Categories & sub-types · Custom fields · Macros. _SLA & escalation_: SLA policies · Business hours & holidays · Escalation rules. _People & clients_: Teams & members · Staff · Clients & sites. _Communication_: Email templates · Help center. _System_: General settings · Audit log.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |

| E15 | **Help center**: author, publish/unpublish, archive/restore and reorder articles with a preview. The portal help-center page stays Phase 5.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| E16 | **Live New Ticket preview** while editing a category or its fields: a slim read-only renderer (sub-type select, priority cards with targets, custom fields) built from the draft, beside the form in the wide sheet — not the real modal.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| E17 | **Audit log** (append-only): every admin write records actor, action (`CREATE`, `UPDATE`, `ARCHIVE`, `RESTORE`, `REORDER`, plus `DELETE` for join rows, `APPROVE`, `REJECT`), entity, entity id and label, request id, and a before/after of the **changed fields only**, in the **same transaction** as the change; secrets never stored (webhook → host only), phone numbers masked. Filterable, cursor-paged, read-only.                                                                                                                                                                |
| E18 | **Live refresh**: after commit the API bumps a config **version** (a generation key that also guards the cache against a stale re-fill) and broadcasts `config:updated {scope, version}` to **all** sockets. The web maps scopes to query keys (form config, form contexts, requester search, macros, teams, ticket details for `p1Chain`, admin lists), refetches until it sees the new version, and refetches the form config after a reconnect. An open New Ticket form keeps the user's input; if their selected category / sub-type was archived, it is cleared with a polite notice. |
| E19 | **Lost updates**: every edit sends the `updatedAt` it started from; a stale one is refused with 409 "Someone else changed this — reload". Prisma unique / not-found / foreign-key errors map to 409 / 404 / 409, never 500.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| E20 | **Admin API**: `/admin/*` routes, bearer-token auth like the rest of the API (CSRF-safe by design; documented), OpenAPI tag `admin`, zod contracts in `packages/shared/src/schemas/admin/*`, per-user write rate limit.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |

## Data model (one migration `phase4_admin`)

- `Category.assetPlaceholderAr String?` (Phase 2 carry-over).
- `AuditAction` += `DELETE`, `APPROVE`, `REJECT`; `AdminAuditLog` += `entityLabel String?`, `requestId String?`,
  `@@index([action, createdAt])`.
- Custom-field options keep living in `CustomField.options` (JSON) and gain `isActive` per option (shared schema; no
  column).
- Anything else a lane truly needs goes into this same migration through the foundation lane (ask in the report).

## Build plan (lanes)

1. **Foundation** — migration + shared admin contracts for every section; API admin plumbing (route plugin with
   authorization helpers, audit writer with diff + redaction, config version + generation-guarded cache,
   `config:updated` broadcast, Prisma error mapping, `expectedUpdatedAt` concurrency, write rate limit, reorder
   helper, staff directory endpoint for pickers); web admin kit (grouped nav, admin table, wide sheet, bilingual
   field, language switch, reorder list with keyboard buttons, "who can see this" note, empty/error states,
   `config:updated` scope → query-key map, reconnect refetch, the stranded-selection notice on the New Ticket form).
2. **Section lanes** (each builds its API + web + tests + EN/AR strings):
   - _Ticket setup_: categories & sub-types (with E16 preview), custom fields (option archive; custom-field display on
     tickets per E6), macros (with the Team Lead rules).
   - _SLA & escalation + General settings_: SLA policies, business hours & holidays, escalation rules (E13 re-timing),
     general settings (E11 rescheduling), configured business-day length (E12).
   - _People & clients_: teams & members (E5, E7, masked webhook, on-call), staff (E9), clients & sites (E10, pending
     sign-ups), archived-team handling in the console (E6).
   - _Communication & system_: email templates (E14), help center (E15), audit log viewer (E17).
3. **Verify** — authorization (every route × role, Team Lead scope), data integrity (archive guards, concurrency,
   cache version race, realtime refresh), UI (a11y, RTL, 390/1280 px, forced colours, keyboard reorder).
4. **Integrate** — close gaps, full checks twice, E2E twice (incl. the done-when spec), screenshots for owner review.

## Tests

- **API:** every admin route: 401/403 per role (incl. a Team Lead on another team, a global macro, super-admin-only
  fields), validation errors, archive guards, `expectedUpdatedAt` 409, audit rows written in the same transaction
  (rolled back with a failed change), cache version bump + `config:updated` emitted after commit, escalation
  re-timing, settings rescheduling, staff role change revoking sessions, pending sign-up approval.
- **Web:** component tests for the kit (bilingual field, reorder by keyboard, sheet focus handling), each section's
  table/sheet (create, edit, archive/restore, reorder, errors), scope → query-key invalidation, the stranded-selection
  notice.
- **E2E (Playwright, two browser contexts):** a client has the New Ticket form open; the Super Admin creates a category
  with its first sub-type and a custom field linked to it; the open form shows them without a reload; the client
  raises a ticket with them; the admin archives them; the ticket still renders them with an "archived" marker. The
  e2e teardown removes the config rows it created and busts the config cache. It matches exactly the key shape the
  spec generates (`e2e-<Date.now()>` for the category, `e2e-<Date.now()>-panel` for the field), removes no macros,
  and keeps a category or field that a remaining ticket still uses.
